HOW UPLOADING WORKS

  1. Choose the file — PDF, DOCX or TXT, up to 20 MB.
  2. Add a clear title (optional) — for example "HR Handbook 2026". Users will see this title in the sources of every answer.
  3. Click Upload and process — the document's status on the knowledge page moves from Pending to Processing to Processed on its own.
  4. Test the assistant — ask a question the new document answers; the reply should cite it under Sources.
